# Worldwide geographic catalogue

Derived from Countries States Cities Database by dr5hn and contributors:
https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database
Source: json/countries+states+cities.json, retrieved 20 October 2026.
Licensed under Open Database License (ODbL); see GEOGRAPHY-LICENSE.txt.

This derivative keeps country, state/region and locality names only, grouped by their parents.
250 countries/territories, 5,268 state/region entries and 148,225 locality names.
Coverage is not exhaustive: some territories have no subdivision or locality entries.
No subdivisions or localities are invented to fill gaps. Names reflect the source, not artisan availability.
