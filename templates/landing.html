A clearer way through the details

Good work starts with a good conversation.

The platform keeps the conversation, offer, booking and payment steps connected.

01

Find a place to begin

Browse artisans and active service listings without an account. Sign-in is required only for private conversations and service requests.

02

Talk through the work

Discuss scope and timing in conversation. The artisan can send an offer; the customer can review, accept, decline or counter it.

03

Agree before payment

Accepting an offer creates the booking and makes the payment step available. The artisan may start once the payment is verified and held.

Details, kept together

Make the scope clear before the work begins.

Use the conversation to explain what needs doing and ask questions. An offer can set out labour, materials, scope and timing so both sides can review the same details before a booking is accepted.

Payment follows agreement

Pay after an offer is accepted.

The payment step follows an accepted offer and booking—not the first conversation.

  1. 01

    After payment, the provider verifies the transaction. A verified payment is held while the booking is underway.

  2. 02

    The artisan can start after the payment is held, then submit the work for completion review.

  3. 03

    The customer confirms completion separately. Confirmation starts payout-release processing; eligible disputes can pause the relevant funds.
